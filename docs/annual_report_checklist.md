# Annual Report Extraction Checklist

Use this checklist to track progress extracting KAMs from annual reports.

**Source:** Tadawul - https://www.saudiexchange.sa/wps/portal/saudiexchange/newsandreports/reports-publications/issuer-reports

**Instructions:**
1. Go to Tadawul → News & Reports → Issuer Reports
2. Search by company name or ticker
3. Download the Annual Report (English if available)
4. Extract KAMs from the Independent Auditor's Report section
5. Mark as complete below

## KAM Categories to Extract

For each annual report, look for these 5 KAM types in the auditor's report:

| Category | Variable | Keywords to Look For |
|----------|----------|---------------------|
| Going Concern | `kam_going_concern` | viability, refinancing, covenant breach, liquidity |
| Revenue | `kam_revenue` | revenue recognition, contract revenue, percentage of completion |
| Assets | `kam_assets` | impairment, goodwill, fair value, inventory valuation |
| Liabilities | `kam_liabilities` | provisions, contingent liabilities, debt, pension |
| Other | `kam_other` | IT systems, acquisitions, related party, regulatory |
